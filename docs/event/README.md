# Trichollective Ireland: QR code and print kit

Both QR codes here open `https://www.trichollective.net/ireland?utm_source=ireland`. Everyone who joins from them is counted as "ireland" in Studio, under Members, where people came from. Both files were decoded after they were generated to check the address is exact.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `ireland-qr.png` | Slides and screens. It is 2048 by 2048 pixels, black on white, with a quiet border built in. |
| `ireland-qr.svg` | Print or design software. It stays sharp at any size. |

## Putting the QR code on a slide

1. Insert `ireland-qr.png` as a picture in Keynote, PowerPoint or Google Slides.
2. Make it at least a third of the slide's height, so people at the back of the room can scan it.
3. Keep the white border around the code, and do not place it over a photo or a coloured background.
4. Put the short address under it in large type, so people can type it if their camera will not scan: **trichollective.net/ireland**
5. Scan it from the back of the room with your own phone before the first talk.

A line to show beside it: "Scan the code to join as a founding member today, or to add your practice to the directory for free."

## The printed poster and table cards

Open **https://www.trichollective.net/ireland/print** on a laptop and print at 100% scale on A4, with no margins.

- Page one is the A4 poster.
- Page two holds two A5 table cards. Cut along the dashed line.

The QR codes on that page are drawn by the site, so they always carry the right address and source.

## The short address

**trichollective.net/ireland** is the address to say from the stage and to print anywhere a QR code will not fit. The older address, `/dublin`, redirects to it permanently, so anything already printed still works.

## Need a different size?

The site makes QR codes for its own pages: `https://www.trichollective.net/qr?path=/ireland&src=ireland` returns an SVG, and adding `&format=png&size=2048` returns a PNG of that width (up to 4096 pixels).
