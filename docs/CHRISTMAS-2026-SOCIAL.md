# 12 Days of Christmas 2026: social campaign brief

Handover for the social agent. Everything here is agreed unless it is marked **TBC**.

## What the campaign is

Trichollective's **12 Days of Christmas** is a gift a day for hair and scalp professionals, from **Saturday 12 to Wednesday 23 December 2026**. The doors live inside **the Christmas edition of Trichozette** (Edition 10, out Tuesday 1 December). One door opens each morning at midnight UK time. The short link **trichollective.net/christmas** takes people straight to the doors in the edition. Behind each door is an offer from a brand we trust, something to learn, or a little help for the year ahead.

Social runs for **the whole of December, one post a day (31 posts)**, in three phases:

| Phase | Dates | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Countdown | 1 to 11 Dec | Build anticipation, grow free accounts and email sign-ups before the first door. |
| 2. The 12 days | 12 to 23 Dec | Reveal that day's door and send people to open and claim it. |
| 3. Thank you | 24 to 31 Dec | Last chance to claim, thank the community, look ahead to 2027. |

## The one action we want

**Open the door and claim the gift on the website.** Codes are never written in a post, a caption, a story or a comment. People claim on the site, which is how we count claims for each brand and how free accounts grow.

How a claim works (use this wording when explaining it):

1. Open today's door in the Christmas edition of Trichozette (trichollective.net/christmas).
2. Sign in or create a free account, then press **Claim this gift**.
3. Your code appears straight away, and we email you a copy to keep.

Gifts are free for everyone with a Trichollective account, including free accounts.

## Links and tracking

Always use the tracked link for the platform, so the Studio report (`/studio/christmas`) can show where claims came from.

- Countdown and thank-you posts: `https://www.trichollective.net/christmas?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=12-days`
- A specific door (opens that door automatically): `https://www.trichollective.net/christmas?door=N&utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=12-days`
- Swap `instagram` for `facebook`, `linkedin` or `tiktok` as needed. Always use `www`.
- On Instagram, put the link in bio and stories. In the caption say "link in bio".

## Voice and copy rules

- Write in Karley's voice: warm, professional, first person plural ("we").
- **Every headline is one complete sentence that states a concrete benefit.** No fragment headlines, no puns, no cute lines.
- Lead with what the professional gets, in the language trichologists and salon professionals use themselves.
- British English. No hashtag walls: three to five relevant tags at most (for example #trichology #hairloss #scalphealth #hairprofessionals #trichollective).
- Do not make clinical claims for a product or treatment. Describe the offer, not the outcome.
- Do not promise a brand's door before the day it opens, and do not name brands still marked **TBC**.
- Mark brand posts as a partner offer (Instagram "Paid partnership" label where the brand agrees, otherwise "Partner offer" in the caption).

## Look and feel

Match the calendar in the Christmas edition, which is in Trichollective's own black, white and paper. Do not introduce gold, red or green as campaign colours: the only colour is each partner's own, behind their door.

- Night scene in ink black (`#0B0B0B`) with soft white falling snow.
- Doors are paper white (`#FBFAF7`) with a black hairline frame and a large black italic numeral; a door that has not opened yet is a white outline in the dark.
- Headlines in the Trichozette Didone (Bodoni Moda), often italic; small labels in spaced capitals; body in the reading serif (Source Serif 4).
- The door swings open on its left hinge to the partner's colour and logo, with white light and sparks.

- Daily door reveal: a short reel or animated post of the numbered door swinging open to the brand's colour and logo, with the offer headline. Screen-record the real door on `/christmas?day=N` (staff preview, signed in) if that is quickest.
- Brand colours for the open door: Secret Hair Solutions deep green `#29382F` with blush `#EBCCC2`; PRP Pure dark red `#3A0D0D` with their red `#FF0000`; Kiera SI black `#0B0B0B`; Little Lady Locks pink `#D4007A`.
- Use each brand's own logo unaltered. Ask before using a brand's product photography.
